## What does this blog name ‘Solsist’s Strobo’ mean?
**Solsist** is my nickname, derived from the Chinese character **“昱”** (yù) in my name, which combines **“日”** (sun) and **“立”** (to stand). I reinterpreted it into **“sol”** (Latin for “sun”) and **“sist”** (from the Latin verb *sistere*, meaning “to stand”).

**Strobo** is short for *stroboscope*.

## What do all these non-English words mean?
These are languages I have studied, or at least dabbled in a little, and all of them are FASCINATING.

**ὅτι ἃ μὴ οἶδα οὐδὲ οἴομαι εἰδέναι**  
Ancient Greek — roughly, *“What I do not know, I do not think I know.”*  
A line associated with Socrates in Plato's *Apology*.

**c'est moi**  
French — **“this is me”**

**scriptum**

**hsiao³-shuo¹**
